Ultimate Double Chocolate Fudge Cake

A rich, moist chocolate cake layered with a glossy dark chocolate fudge glaze and fresh raspberries. The perfect centerpiece for celebrations.

Bake Time 30 mins
Servings 8 servings
Difficulty Easy-Medium

Follow these steps carefully for the perfect fluffy and moist chocolate cake crumb.

  1. 1

    Prep & Preheat Oven

    10 mins

    Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Butter and lightly flour two 9-inch round cake pans. Line the bottoms with parchment paper for easy removal later.

  2. 2

    Whisk Dry Ingredients

    5 mins

    In a large mixing bowl, combine the flour, sugar, cocoa powder, baking powder, baking soda, and fine sea salt. Whisk vigorously until the mixture is uniform and lump-free.

  3. 3

    Incorporate Wet Ingredients

    5 mins

    Add the eggs, whole milk, vegetable oil, and vanilla extract to the dry mixture. Beat with an electric mixer on medium speed for about 2 minutes until smooth and slightly thickened.

  4. 4

    Bloom Cocoa with Boiling Water

    5 mins

    Reduce mixer speed to low and slowly stream in the boiling water (or hot coffee, which intensifies chocolate flavour). Mix until combined. The batter will be extremely thin and liquid; this is expected!

  5. 5

    Pour and Bake

    30 mins

    Divide the batter evenly between your two prepared cake pans. Bake on the middle rack for 30 to 35 minutes, or until a wooden toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs.

  6. 6

    Cool, Glaze, & Frost

    15 mins

    Cool the cakes in their pans for 10 minutes, then invert onto wire cooling racks to cool completely. Heat heavy cream until steaming, pour over chocolate chips, let sit for 2 minutes, then whisk into a glossy glaze. Pour glaze over cooled cake layers and garnish with raspberries.
